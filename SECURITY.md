# Security policy

Please do not disclose a security vulnerability in a public issue. Contact the
DocYantra maintainers through the repository's private security channel with
the affected version, a minimal reproduction, and the impact. Do not attach
customer documents or credentials.

The project is licensed under AGPL-3.0-only. Security reports and fixes do not
grant Enterprise or other commercial rights; those are available only through a
separate signed agreement.

Until a fix is available, keep details private and avoid publishing exploit
material. We will acknowledge receipt and coordinate a fix or mitigation.
