# Contributor License Agreements

The individual and corporate CLA drafts in this directory are counsel-review
documents. They are not legal advice and are not in force until counsel has
approved the text and the project activates a signature and enforcement
workflow.

The counterparty for these drafts is Sublime Innovation Technologies Limited;
DocYantra is the project/product name used in the individual and corporate
forms.

The intended grant lets the project distribute contributions under the
AGPL-3.0-only license and separately grant commercial licenses under a signed
agreement. It does not itself create an Enterprise agreement.
