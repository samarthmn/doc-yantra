Contributions.

## 1. Definitions

**"You"** or **"Your"** means the Corporation entering this Agreement and
entities that control, are controlled by, or are under common control with it.
"Control" means direct or indirect power to direct management, ownership of at
least fifty percent of outstanding voting interests, or equivalent beneficial
ownership.

**"Contribution"** means each original work of authorship expressly identified
in Schedule B and each original work of authorship, including source code,
object code, documentation, designs, tests, or modifications or additions to
an existing work, intentionally submitted by You or by an employee listed in
Schedule A to the Company for inclusion in or documentation of the DocYantra
Project (the "Work").

**"Submit"** means any electronic, verbal, or written communication sent to
the Company or its representatives for the purpose of discussing or improving the
Work, including pull requests, commits, patches, issue comments, and mailing-list
messages. A communication conspicuously marked in writing as "Not a
Contribution" is excluded.
